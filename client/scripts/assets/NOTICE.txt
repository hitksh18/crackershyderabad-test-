Each file has been subset to the sixteen characters an invoice amount can
contain (" ,-./0123456789" plus U+20B9 RUPEE SIGN); no other modification was
made. The subsets are embedded, base64-encoded, in src/utils/invoiceAssets.js
and are therefore redistributed in the client bundle — hence this notice, and
hence the full OFL text shipping next to it, which clause 2 of that licence
requires of any copy of the font.

Noto Sans declares no Reserved Font Name — its copyright line carries no "with
Reserved Font Name" clause — so clause 3 imposes no rename, and these subsets
may keep the Noto Sans name. (A font that does reserve its name, which most
OFL families do, could not: a subset is a Modified Version, and clause 3 bars
those from using the reserved name. Worth knowing before swapping this font.)

Noto Sans was chosen because it has a real U+20B9 glyph, which is the entire
reason a font is embedded here at all, and because its digits are tabular —
all ten share one advance, in both weights, so the Rate and Amount columns
align down the page and a bold total row still lines up with the regular rows
above it. Lato was requested first and could not be used: it has no rupee sign
in either weight, so every price would have printed a blank .notdef box.
